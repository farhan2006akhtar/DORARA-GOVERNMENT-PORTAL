Foundational law

Constitution & Rules

A starting framework for Dorara's fictional constitutional government.

Foundational principles

⚖

Constitutional supremacy

All government offices and public institutions operate under the constitution of Dorara.

♙

Separation of powers

The executive, legislature and judiciary have distinct responsibilities and oversight functions.

◈

Public finance accountability

Public funds must be authorised, documented and subject to independent audit.

◎

Equal citizenship

All citizens are entitled to equal protection and fair access to public services.

▤

Ministerial responsibility

Each minister is responsible for departmental delivery and regular reporting to the Prime Minister and Parliament.

These are draft roleplay principles, not a legally operative constitution. Your group can amend them by agreement.

Live launch requirements

①

Verified individual accounts

Each participant needs their own email login and password reset process. Never share the President's login.

②

Secure shared database

Store members, ministries, budgets, proposals, votes and notices centrally so every authorised user sees the same records.

③

Server-side permissions

Enforce role access in the database and server, not only by hiding buttons in the browser.

④

Audit log & backups

Record who submitted, edited or approved each decision, with timestamps and recoverable backups.
